Privacy Policy

Block Puzzle: Jungle Crush

Effective date: October 9, 2026

BCS Studio ("we", "us" or "our") develops and publishes Block Puzzle: Jungle Crush (the "Game"). This policy explains how information is handled when you play the Android Game, use its advertising features or contact us.

You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number or date of birth to play. The Game stores progress locally. Advertising services may still process technical information, as explained below.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game stores the following information locally to provide its features:

  • Saved boards, scores, level progress and game settings.
  • Language, sound, music, vibration and reduced-motion preferences.
  • Tutorial status and your selected age category, including a choice not to state your age.
  • A previously saved remove-ads entitlement, if present.

The gameplay system does not upload your saved boards or scores to a BCS Studio account server. Android backup and restore settings may affect local data; see Section 6.

Google Play Games sign-in, leaderboards, achievements and cloud-save features are disabled in this version. The Game does not currently offer in-app purchases or collect payment details.

2. Advertising and technical information

The Game uses Google AdMob. Ads may include a bottom banner, an occasional full-screen ad at the end of a completed game, and an optional rewarded ad that lets you continue when no moves remain. You choose whether to watch a rewarded ad. An unavailable ad does not prevent you from starting a new game.

Google's advertising software receives and processes information to deliver ads, measure their operation and detect fraud. Depending on the SDK version and privacy configuration, this can include:

  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate a general location.
  • Advertising and app interactions, such as ad views, video views and taps.
  • Technical and diagnostic information about app and advertising performance.
  • App or device identifiers, such as App Set ID, where processed by the SDK.

This information is not necessarily anonymous. The Game is configured not to transmit the Android Advertising ID through its Android permission and child-directed advertising settings. These settings and the disabling of ad personalization do not eliminate all advertising-related data processing.

For more information about Google's data handling, read the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

The Game does not request device-location permissions. A general location inferred from an IP address is different from access to your device's GPS or location services. The Game also does not request access to your contacts, camera or microphone.

3. Children and family advertising settings

The Game uses child-directed, non-personalized ad requests and a maximum G ad-content rating for all players. Publisher ad personalization and publisher first-party identifiers are disabled. Selecting an older age category does not enable personalized advertising.

The Game does not ask children to create an account or provide identifying information to play. Children and players whose age is unknown receive the same child-directed advertising configuration. Advertising services may still process technical information for delivering ads, security and fraud prevention.

A parent or guardian may contact us to ask about a child's information or request review or deletion of information provided directly to BCS Studio. Please avoid sending unnecessary information about a child. If you believe a child has sent us personal information that should be removed, email batikansavci@gmail.com.

4. Privacy messages and advertising choices

The Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to determine whether a privacy message or privacy choice is required. Advertising requests wait until that platform permits them. Where available, you can reopen privacy options from the Game's settings. External privacy links and privacy controls may be protected by a parent gate.

Advertising services may use local storage or similar technologies to operate, prevent fraud and remember privacy choices. Their use is subject to the applicable privacy settings and Google's policies.

Declining to watch an optional rewarded ad does not stop you from starting a new game. A previously saved remove-ads entitlement suppresses banner and interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads remain a separate choice.

5. Information you send when contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include, such as a description of a problem or an attached screenshot. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue and handle your request. Our email provider also processes correspondence as part of delivering and storing email.

Only send information needed for your request. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information about yourself or a child.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data

Progress and preferences remain on your device until they are changed or removed. You can delete local game data through Android's app settings by selecting the Game and clearing its storage or app data. This removes saved progress and preferences. Menu names vary by device.

Uninstalling normally removes the Game's local app data. Android backup or restore features may restore some data after reinstallation, depending on your device settings. The Game's Play Games age and connection-consent preference file is excluded from its Android backup and device-transfer configuration. BCS Studio cannot remotely erase a saved board stored only on your device.

Support correspondence

We retain support correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, handle related follow-up or disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing us. Where information must be retained for a legitimate legal reason, we will explain the relevant limitation when responding to your request.

Google service data

Google determines retention for information processed by its services under its own policies. Clearing the Game's storage does not necessarily delete information already processed by Google. See How Google retains data and the controls described in Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Data security

Game progress and preferences are stored in the application's local storage, which is subject to Android's app-access protections and your device's security settings.

Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). See Google's SDK security disclosure. Email security also depends on the email services used to send and receive a message. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your requests and choices

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to its use, or withdraw consent where processing depends on consent. Contact us using the email below for requests concerning information held by BCS Studio. We may ask for only the information reasonably needed to verify and respond to your request.

For information handled by Google, use the privacy controls and contact options described in Google's Privacy Policy. You may also have the right to contact your local data protection authority. There is no Game account to delete in this version.

9. Third-party services and links

Google AdMob and its privacy-message services handle the advertising-related information described above. If you tap an ad or open an external link, the destination's own privacy policy applies to activity on that service.
