Privacy policy

A clear guide to your drawings, advertising and privacy choices.

Effective & updated: October 2, 2026Android · com.RBGames.minocolor

English

Your artwork stays on your device. No account is required. The game includes advertising, so Google may process advertising and technical data. Children receive the protections described below.

1. Who we are

RBGames ("we", "us") provides MinoColor: Coloring Game (the "Game"). This policy covers the Android Game, support communications and this policy page. Privacy contact: batikansavci@gmail.com.

2. Drawings and game information

Your coloring progress, saved pictures, album information and language, music and sound preferences are stored locally. We do not operate a gameplay account or cloud gallery, and the Game does not upload your drawings to us. There is no public profile, chat or user-to-user messaging.

The Game does not request your name, date of birth, email address or precise location. It does not access your contacts, microphone, camera or existing photo library. We have not enabled Unity Analytics or a separate gameplay analytics service.

3. Advertising and Google's services

We use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) for banner and interstitial ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable privacy messages and choices. Banner ads appear in menus; full-screen ads may appear at breaks after leaving a drawing.

Google's SDK may process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device/app details, ad and app interactions, performance and diagnostic information, and identifiers permitted by the SDK configuration. These support ad delivery and measurement, service reliability, security and fraud prevention. Our Android build removes advertising-ID and Android AdServices permissions; the publisher first-party ID is disabled. These restrictions do not eliminate all SDK data processing.

Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit. Google may process it outside your country under its policies and applicable safeguards. Details: Google Privacy Policy, Google advertising and SDK data disclosure.

4. Children and families

The Game is intended for families, including children. We apply child-directed ad treatment to all players, request the general-audience (G) ad content rating, and mark users as under the age of consent in UMP. We do not request interest-based personalized advertising or remarketing. Google explains these protections here.

We do not ask children to provide personal information to play. Advertising still involves limited technical processing described above. A parent or guardian can contact us about a child's information, request review or deletion of information we hold, or report an inappropriate ad. Please avoid sending a child's name, photo or other unnecessary personal details.

5. Privacy choices

Under Settings → Privacy, you can open this policy and advertising privacy options when Google makes them available. Some options or consent forms may not appear because child-directed settings apply. Changing a privacy choice does not switch the Game to personalized ads. You may also use your device's privacy controls.

6. Saving and sharing pictures

When you choose to save a picture, the Game exports its own PNG image. On Android 10 and later, it writes to the Pictures/MinoColor folder without requesting broad storage or photo access. On older supported devices, the system share/save interface is used.

Sharing happens only after you select a destination in the system share sheet. The receiving app or service then handles the picture under its own policy. Exported pictures may be synced by a gallery or backup service you enable; we do not control that service.

7. Sharing and support

We do not sell personal information or build advertising profiles from your artwork. Google processes advertising data as described above. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send through our email provider, and use it to respond and resolve your request. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect rights and security.

8. Retention, deletion and your rights

Local progress and settings remain until you clear the Game's app data or uninstall it, subject to any device backup/restore settings. Saved gallery images and copies shared elsewhere remain until you delete them separately. We cannot remotely access or erase your local drawings.

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Google's retention and deletion rules apply to data it processes. Depending on your location, you or a guardian may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, withdraw applicable consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us for data we hold; use Google's privacy controls for data Google controls. We may need proportionate information to verify a request.

9. Security and this website

We limit access and data use to the purposes described here, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. This page has no advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts or tracking cookies added by us. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process connection information, including IP addresses, to provide and secure hosting; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Updates and contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the Game or data practices and revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated where required.
