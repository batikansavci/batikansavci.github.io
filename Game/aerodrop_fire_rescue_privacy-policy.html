1. Overview

AeroDrop: Fire Rescue does not require an account. BcsStudio does not independently operate a server that stores your gameplay or profile data. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. The game displays ads through Google Mobile Ads, which may collect and share certain data as described below.

This Privacy Policy explains how BcsStudio ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with AeroDrop: Fire Rescue (the "Game").

3. Advertising and third-party services

The Game uses Google Mobile Ads to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. According to Google's disclosures, the SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available;
  • user interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad views, and video views; and
  • diagnostic information, such as app or SDK performance, crashes, launch time, and energy usage.

This information may be used by Google for advertising, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Google states that Google Mobile Ads data is encrypted in transit. Google's handling of data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information for advertising
  • Google Mobile Ads data disclosure

4. Consent and advertising choices

Where required by law, the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and record your advertising consent choices before requesting ads. The ads available to you may depend on your region, age eligibility, device settings, and consent choices.

You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID and manage ad privacy options through your Android device settings. You may also manage Google ad preferences through My Ad Center.

5. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising and technical data may be shared with Google as necessary to provide, measure, secure, and prevent fraud in advertising. We do not use your locally stored gameplay data for independent advertising profiles.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally stored game progress and settings remain on your device until you clear the Game's app data or uninstall the Game. Because the Game does not provide user accounts and we do not independently store this local data on our servers, we cannot remotely retrieve or delete it.

Data processed by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's policies and your Google or device privacy controls.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum digital-consent age in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the Game, please contact us so that we can review the request.

8. Security

We limit the Game's own storage to local gameplay data and do not require an account. Information transmitted by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit according to Google's SDK disclosure. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning access, deletion, correction, objection, restriction, or consent withdrawal. For locally stored game data, you can clear the Game's app data or uninstall it. For data controlled by Google, use your Google and Android privacy controls or contact Google. You may contact us regarding questions about our own practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its services, or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated version on this page and change the effective date above.
